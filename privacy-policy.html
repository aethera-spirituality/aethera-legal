Aethera Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026 · Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. Who we are

Aethera Spirituality (“Aethera”, bundle identifier com.aethera.spirituality) is an iOS application published by Viktoriia Lizunova (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal data the app handles, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the app only, not to any other product or website.

For data protection purposes, Viktoriia Lizunova is the controller of the personal data described below. You can reach us at vik.liz97@atomicmail.io.

2. The short version

  • Your account is anonymous. It is created for you automatically on first launch and holds no name, email address or password of the kind you would sign in with.
  • Your profile, readings, chat history and notes are stored on our servers, which are located in the European Union, so they are there when you come back.
  • Photographs you submit for a reading are processed to produce that reading and then discarded. We do not keep them.
  • Some features send your request to an AI provider to generate the result. Section 7 says exactly what is sent.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, there are no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the app, and we do not sell your data.
  • You can ask us what we hold about you and ask us to delete it.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Your profile — the name you enter, your date of birth, the gender you select, and the intentions you choose during onboarding. If you add them later, also the time and the place of your birth. Birth details are what the astrological content is calculated from.
  • Content you create — journal entries, descriptions of your dreams, the messages you send in the chat feature, reflections you write on the cards you draw, affirmations you keep, sound mixes you build, and the items you mark as favourites.
  • Photographs — if you use a feature that reads an image, the photo of your palm or of the night sky that you take or choose. The image is used to produce that one reading and is not stored: it exists only for the duration of the request that carries it. What survives the request is a one-way fingerprint of the image, its size and format, and the automated verdict on whether the frame was usable at all.
  • Details about other people — the compatibility feature asks you for another person’s name and date of birth, the kind of relationship you have with them, and optionally their place of birth. Only enter someone else’s details if you are entitled to do so. We handle that information the same way we handle yours, and it is deleted together with your account.
  • Support messages — if you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • An anonymous account identifier — on first launch the app creates an account for you from a random value generated on your device and kept in the device keychain. There is no name, email address or password attached to it, and we cannot connect it to your identity. It is what lets your readings and notes still be there the next time you open the app.
  • Technical request data — when the app talks to our servers we process the request itself, the app version, the operating system version, the device model and the IP address the request comes from. IP addresses are used for delivery and abuse prevention, not to build a profile of you.
  • Purchase status — whether a purchase or subscription is active, and the transaction identifiers Apple gives us. We never receive your card number or full billing details.
  • Usage counters — how many times you have used features that are limited on the free plan, so that the limits shown in the app are the real ones.

3.3 Information from third parties

Apple tells us whether a purchase succeeded and whether a subscription is active, and sends us notifications about renewals, cancellations and refunds for that subscription. Aside from the services listed in section 6, we do not buy or receive personal data about you from anyone.

4. Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when a feature needs it, and iOS shows you the reason at that moment. You can withdraw any permission in Settings › Aethera; the feature that relied on it stops working, the rest of the app keeps working.

7. Features powered by AI models

Several features send your request to OpenAI to generate a response. What leaves our servers for that purpose is: the birth details and preferences in your profile; the birth details you entered for another person, when the feature is about the two of you; the answers you gave in that feature’s questionnaire; the message you typed in the chat; the text you wrote about a dream; and the photograph you submitted for a palm or night-sky reading.

We send the minimum needed to produce the result, and your account identifier is not sent with the request — the provider receives the content and nothing that ties it to you. We do not use your content to train models, and we have not agreed to let OpenAI train on it either. Even so, do not put information into these features that you would not want processed by a third-party service.

8. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and receives your payment details; we receive only the purchase status and Apple’s transaction identifiers, which is what lets the app unlock paid features and restore them on a new device. Apple’s handling of your payment data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

9. Tracking and advertising

The app contains no advertising SDKs, does not read Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA), does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not build advertising profiles. It also contains no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Because of that, the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

10. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We disclose data only to the service providers listed above, when the law requires it (a valid legal request), to protect our rights or someone’s safety, or to a successor if the app changes hands — in which case this policy continues to apply until you are told otherwise.

11. International transfers

Our servers and our database are located in the European Union. One provider, OpenAI, processes requests in the United States; that transfer relies on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. Apple processes purchase data under its own terms and transfer mechanisms. Ask us at vik.liz97@atomicmail.io if you want the specifics for a given provider.

12. How long we keep data

Your profile and the content you create are kept for as long as your account exists, so that you can come back to them. When the account is deleted, the profile, readings, chat history, journal and dream entries, the people you saved for compatibility, your settings, your entitlements and your usage counters are erased from our database. What remains is a minimal record that the account existed, with no personal data in it: it stops a device from silently reappearing as the old account and lets us reconcile purchase notifications that Apple may still send about a past subscription. Server request logs are short-lived and kept only to operate and secure the service. Photographs you submit for a reading are never stored at all (see section 3).

13. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to systems that hold personal data is limited to those who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep the amount of data we hold as small as the app allows — that is the strongest protection we can offer.

14. Your rights and choices

  • Access — ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix data that is wrong.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your data.
  • Objection and restriction — object to processing based on legitimate interests, or ask us to pause it.
  • Portability — receive a copy of the data you gave us in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time; this does not affect what happened before.

Write to vik.liz97@atomicmail.io and we will respond within 30 days.

Deleting your account. Because the account is anonymous, the one thing that identifies it is the User ID shown on the Settings screen in the app, where a Copy button puts it on your clipboard. Email that identifier to vik.liz97@atomicmail.io and ask us to delete the account; we erase everything listed in section 12. Deleting the app from your device does not by itself delete the data held on our servers.

If you are in the EEA or the UK and you think we have handled your data wrongly, you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

California residents. You have the right to know what personal information we collect and why, to request deletion or correction, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so no “Do Not Sell or Share” mechanism is needed. To exercise a right, email vik.liz97@atomicmail.io; an authorised agent may act for you with written proof.

15. Children

The app is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email vik.liz97@atomicmail.io and we will delete it.
